About

Welcome to Little Wonders Auguston! Opened in 2015, we are in the Auguston neighbourhood of Abbotsford, and love to go for walks on nearby forest trails and visit the neighbourhood parks, especially the spray park in summer! Our location has many different programs to offer. From Infant Toddler, 30 Months to Five years, Montessori Preschool, Academy (our Kindergarten Prep program) to School Age, we have something for every age group.

Address

36363 Auguston Parkway South, Abbotsford

Programs

Infant Toddler (12 spaces; 3 months to 36 Months)

Daycare (25 spaces; 30 Months to 5 yrs old)

School Age (36 spaces; Kindergarten to Grade 5; Auguston Elementary)

Academy (10 spaces; 4 hour, 5 mornings a week program for children 4 to 5 years old)

Montessori Preschool (40 spaces; morning or afternoon 2.5 hour program for ages 3-5 yrs old)
