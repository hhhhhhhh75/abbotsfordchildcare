About

Welcome to Little Wonders Calvin! This location is our Christian Faith-based centre. It opened in November 2016. It is upstairs in Calvin Presbyterian Church. We have two Infant Toddler rooms, with combined spaces for 24 children. Our Daycare program has space for 16 children and is a combined Montessori and traditional Play-based Curriculum. At Little Wonders Montessori (Calvin) we want all families and children to feel comfortable and welcome. We do this by getting to know you and your child(ren), having clear open communication, and spending quality time with your child as they participate in our daily activities.
