About

Welcome to Kids Club Delair! At our location, you can expect to see welcoming smiles and children exploring the world through play, creativity and using their imaginations. We strive to have every family feel like they are a part of our big family. In the mornings we run a "preschool-like" program where the children learn through song, dance, story telling, and puppets, as well as time for art, science, math and music/movement. We go outside rain, shine or snow and also have a gymnasium we can use to make sure children have an area to burn off their energy no matter what the weather does!
