About

Little Wonders Montessori on Gladys Avenue is the original Little Wonders location. We have large, oversized classrooms for every age with plenty of space for the children to grow and play. We also have a large outdoor play area with a variety of activities and lots of room to run around. Our Teachers provide a balanced approach to Montessori Curriculum and Traditional Play Based theme teaching.
