About

Welcome to Kids Club Chilliwack! This location opened in 2019. Conveniently located in the heart of Downtown Chilliwack near District 1881 Kids Club Child Care Centre- Chilliwack welcomes children ages 3-5 with limited availability for children as young as 2.5 years old. Our program is play based and takes a blended approach which includes aspects of traditional play based learning, Reggio Emilia inspired learning, and Montessori. Our full day program includes opportunities for both student and teacher led learning including art, science, music, yoga, and an age appropriate introduction to French.
