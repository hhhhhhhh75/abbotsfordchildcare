Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some of our frequently asked questions.

Do you provide Transport to and from elementary school?arrow_back

We do provide transport to and from the schools we service. Each location services different schools in their local community. Some are within walking distance otherwise, Kids Club and Little Wonders provide bus transportation. All our drivers have a valid Class 4 BC Driver's License with a clean Drivers Abstract.

What is your sick child or illness policy?arrow_back

If your child falls ill while at the center we will contact you to pick up your child as soon as possible. The child should remain away from care for a minimum of 48 hours depending on the nature of illness. Referring to our Health and Illness Policy guidelines is always recommended to ensure we are mitigating the spread of illness through our classrooms.

What are your closure dates?arrow_back
  • New Year's Day
  • Family Day
  • Good Friday
  • Victoria Day
  • Canada Day
  • BC Day
  • Labour Day
  • Truth & Reconciliation Day
  • Thanksgiving Day
  • Remembrance Day
  • Christmas Day
  • Christmas Holiday Closure (Dates will be announced by Nov. 1st each year.)

*Please note that if one of these days falls on a weekend, the Centre may be closed either the Friday before or the Monday after the holiday. All Preschool and Academy programs follow the Abbotsford School District Calendar.

Is there a reduction of fees if my child is sick, for holidays, or vacation?arrow_back

There is no refund or credit based on sick days, weather closures, vacations or statutory holidays. Fees are based on available care days throughout the year and then averaged out to be a standard monthly fee.

Can my child start half way through a month?arrow_back

Yes. If a mid-month start date is available for your child you will be prorated for the days in care used for the month.

What is your withdrawal policy?arrow_back

Parents wishing to withdraw their child from the program must provide at least 45 days written notice prior to withdrawal. If sufficient notice is not given, a 1.5 month's fee will be charged in lieu of notice. The deposit will be returned to you within 30 days of the last day of enrollment or taken off the last month's fee. If 45 days notice is not given, your deposit will be held until your fees in lieu are paid. In order to be eligible for a prorated half month fee your child's last day must fall on or before the 15th of the month.

Do you accept the Affordable Child Care Benefit (Subsidy)?arrow_back

Yes, however it is the family's responsibility to apply to the Affordable Child Care Benefit Program and maintain their application status.

Are you a part of the Government Fee Reduction Initiative?arrow_back

Yes. Please email to receive our current fees that show this fee reduction.

How long can I expect to be on the waitlist?arrow_back

Available spaces are unpredictable and therefore a set period of wait time can not be answered. It will depend on withdrawals and program availability. When a spot becomes available you will be contacted directly and given further instruction on how to register. There is no fee to be placed on the waitlist.

Are your programs inclusive?arrow_back

Yes, Little Wonders Montessori and Kids Club Child Care works directly with Supported Child development in each of the communities we services.

Do you offer part-time or casual care?arrow_back

We only provide full time spaces. We do have a drop in rate if ratio allows.

What age groups do you accept?arrow_back

We cater to children aged 4 months until the summer they have completed grade 5.

Do you offer toilet training assistance?arrow_back

Yes, we support children through the toilet training process in partnership with parents.

What are the staff requirements to work in your centres?arrow_back

All our employees have the appropriate documentation associated with working in their specific programs (Such as ECE, ECE-IT, ECEA, RA) in accordance to Fraser Health Licensing regulations. In addition, all employees must have their first aid certification and a clean criminal record check. Little Wonders Montessori and Kids Club Child care provides additional professional development and training for our employees to keep them up to date on certifications and up to date on industry standards.

What is the difference between Little Wonders and Kids Club?arrow_back

Little Wonders and Kids Club are both owned by the same provider, but they offer different approaches to early childhood education to suit varying needs. Little Wonders follows a Montessori approach, which emphasizes hands-on, self-directed learning, fostering independence and critical thinking. In contrast, Kids Club is structured around the Early Learning Framework, which provides a play-based, guided learning experience that supports developmental milestones through structured activities. Both options are excellent, as they cater to different learning styles and preferences. The best choice depends on the child's needs—Montessori may be ideal for children who thrive with independence and exploration, while the Early Learning Framework may better support those who benefit from structured guidance and group-based learning.
